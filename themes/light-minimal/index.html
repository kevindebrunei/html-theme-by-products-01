    Client Services · Provenance

    The Maison Standards

    § 01

    Archival Presentation Packaging

    Every Edition is nestled in a custom matte-black rigid presentation casket, wrapped in gold-debossed archival parchment and sealed with our insignia.

    § 02

    Insured Courier Dispatch

    Complimentary white-glove, insured courier transit on all Editions. Each consignment is tracked with direct provenance documentation from the atelier.

    § 03

    Finite Edition Registry

    Editions are released in strictly numbered seasonal allocations. When a seasonal vault closes, matrices are retired and never mass-reproduced.
